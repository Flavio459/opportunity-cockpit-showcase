Opportunity Cockpit

Intelligent Decision Support System

Helps businesses evaluate market opportunities, compare risks, and make decisions with confidence, organization, and speed. The system gathers scattered information, highlights critical attention points, and maintains a clear audit history of how every decision was reached.

Core remains private
1. Problem

Information Overload & Slow Decisions

Market opportunities arrive scattered across platforms, emails, and briefs with incomplete requirements, hiding risks and slowing down strategic evaluation.

2. Solution

Intelligent Structured Screening

Gathers criteria, normalizes compensation and workload expectations, and verifies factual evidence before surfacing high-confidence recommendations.

3. Business Benefit

Speed, Governance & Error Prevention

Saves 5–10 hours weekly of executive filtering time, flags missing information proactively, and ensures every decision is defensible and traceable.

How It Works

  • AI Decision Support: Extracts semantic criteria from unstructured postings without making ungrounded assumptions.
  • Evidence Verification: Matches project requirements against proven capabilities only when explicit evidence exists.
  • Traceable Audit History: Every metric and recommendation includes an inspection trail of underlying sources.

Enterprise Safety Controls

  • Human-in-the-Loop Gate: External actions (proposals, outreach, agreements) strictly require conscious human authorization.
  • Uncertainty Handling: Missing variables remain explicitly marked as missing rather than being fabricated.
  • Zero Private Leakage: Protected configurations and production models stay sandboxed; demo runs synthetic data.

Human control
